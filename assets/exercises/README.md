# Exercise photographs

Source: [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), revision `f00c92c7dcf1216a928a52c3706c7ce8e2f71ed5`.

Upstream describes this collection as a public-domain dataset and publishes it under the Unlicense (see LICENSE.md). Photos were downloaded unchanged from `exercises/<id>/1.jpg` at that revision. The repository credits wrkout/exercises.json as its original dataset.

`js/exercise-images.js` records the explicit exercise-name mappings. Filenames preserve upstream IDs for attribution. Unknown names receive no photo; do not infer photos from the broader muscle-group aliases in exercise-catalog.js. Related variations are labeled in the expanded photo view.

All photos are bundled and precached for offline use. No third-party image requests are made during app use.
